Application and maintenance

Application must be done by a professional, distributing the pieces according to the hair’s density, area and assessment. Schedule a check-up and maintenance at about 45 days. Do not apply direct heat or oily products to the adhesive bonds.

The number of pairs or “sandwiches” depends on the technique used; with two strips per bond, 20 strips make 10 bonds. Do not confuse the number of strips with the number of pairs.
